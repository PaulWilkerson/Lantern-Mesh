A device-local reader and peer mesh

Keep a little light close.

Lantern lets you capture pages and keep files on your Android phone for offline reading. When you choose, share offered copies with nearby phones over Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi.

Your library stays yours.

Lantern has no account system and no content server. Your captured pages and imported files stay on your phone unless you explicitly offer them on a mesh channel you choose. Capture contacts the URL you enter directly, while Google Play handles the optional mesh subscription.
