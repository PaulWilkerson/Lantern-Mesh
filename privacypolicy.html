Privacy policy

Effective 6 September 2026
Last updated 6 September 2026
Describes app version 1.79 (versionCode 86) and later unless replaced.

This policy is only for the Android app Lantern published as app.lantern. It is not the policy for lantern.io, GetLantern, or “Lantern: Fast & Private VPN” (org.getlantern.lantern).

1. Who we are

Lantern is a device-local offline library, nearby mesh, and optional USB LoRa accessory app. Pages, files, chat, maps, and controls live on your phone. The developer does not operate an account system or a content server for this app.

Contact: lantern.mesh@gmail.com (also the address on the Google Play listing for app.lantern).

2. Short version

  • No Lantern account. No sign-in with us.
  • Library, files, chat, maps, and home controls stay on the phone unless you offer them on mesh or send them on LoRa.
  • We do not run a backend that receives your library, messages, files, or location.
  • Location is read on the device for mesh discovery, map pins, and locking LoRa to the legal band for where GPS says you are. Coordinates are not uploaded to us.
  • The SIM / network country code is read on the device to check LoRa rules. It is not uploaded to us.
  • Capture, weather, satellite scan, and optional AI keys talk to third parties you choose — not to a Lantern server.
  • Google Play processes the optional yearly privacy plan. Home automation is included and is not a purchase in current builds.
  • Paid Bluetooth hops can use AES-256-GCM. LoRa is ordinary radio (compact plaintext).

4. Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, and the app does not send to a Lantern server:

  • Name, email, or phone number as an account (we only see email if you write to us)
  • Payment card details (Google Play handles payment)
  • Address book or SMS
  • Advertising IDs
  • Analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the production app
  • A browsing history other than URLs you choose to capture, stored locally

Location, SIM country, chat, files, and keys stay on the device or go peer-to-peer / to a site or API you invoked. They are not a developer database.

5. Location and radio identifiers

Lantern requests approximate and precise location. That is used on the device for:

  • Nearby Connections — Android requires location for Bluetooth / Wi-Fi peer discovery on many versions.
  • Maps — your last fix for the map cursor and, if you enable it, a pin that mesh friends can see while your mesh is on.
  • LoRa country lock — GPS (and a SIM/network country check) pick the unlicensed band. Mock locations are ignored. Without a recent GPS fix, LoRa will not transmit.

Coordinates are stored locally as the last fix. They are not uploaded to the developer. If you turn on map sharing and mesh, nearby Lantern friends on your channel can see an approximate position. You can turn that off.

Deny location and mesh discovery, maps, and LoRa TX will not work. Capture and reading still work.

USB host permission is used only to talk to a LoRa board you plug in. The app does not inventory other USB devices for us.

6. What leaves the phone

Capture

Saving a page requests that URL (HTTPS when the site supports it). The site, your ISP, and anyone on that path can see the fetch. The user-agent is a generic Android browser plus “Lantern”. Tracking query parameters are stripped from the stored URL when recognized. Tapping a live link opens your system browser.

Weather

If you enable weather in Settings, the app may request a forecast from a public weather endpoint when a network is up, at most once per 12 hours, and save a .txt file in Files. A local notification tells you it arrived.

Satellite kit scan

Optional Wi-Fi scan looks at nearby SSID names for common satellite-kit patterns. SSIDs stay on the device. Associating with a kit is your network, not ours.

Ask / Field AI

If you paste a provider API key and enable live Ask, your question is sent to that provider under their privacy policy. Offline Ask uses a bundled corpus and does not leave the phone. We never receive your keys.

Google Play Billing

Optional product mesh_lock_yearly ($12 / year): AES-256-GCM on Bluetooth mesh hops and optional share-password lock. Google processes the purchase under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app stores local entitlement timestamps (~13 months) so the plan still works without a live Play check. Home automation is included; current builds do not require home_unlock.

Google Play services — Nearby Connections

Start on Mesh advertises and discovers other Lantern phones on the same channel using Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Wi-Fi Direct. Nearby radios — not only Lantern users — may observe that a radio is advertising. Offered titles, files, chat, and optional profile/location go only over accepted links (and over LoRa if you connected a board — see below).

USB LoRa

If you connect a USB LoRa module, attest, and accept the radio disclaimer, compact mesh payloads (chat, hello, catalog, commands) are transmitted on the locked unlicensed band. That is broadcast radio. Anyone with a compatible receiver on that band can demodulate it. LoRa is not covered by the Play encryption plan. Files and full pages stay on Bluetooth.

Home controls

Buttons you create send HTTP or BLE payloads to addresses you typed (your LAN or a paired device). Those packets go to your equipment, not to us.

8. Children

Lantern is not directed at children under 13 (or the digital-consent age in your country). Mesh chat, files, location, and radio are not a children’s product. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Child-safety reports: lantern.mesh@gmail.com (do not email suspected CSAM as an attachment).

9. Retention, Burn, and your controls

  • Delete a page in the reader; remove a file in Files.
  • Stop offering on a page or file.
  • Stop radios with Stop on Mesh; disconnect LoRa in Settings.
  • Turn off map share, profile share, weather, Ask live in Settings.
  • Cancel mesh_lock_yearly in Google Play. Bluetooth encryption stops when the local cache lapses.
  • Uninstall removes remaining app storage on that phone.

Burn on this device

Settings → Burn clears library pages, files, chat, friends, profile, map pins, legend, home controls, LoRa attestation, and the current callsign, then assigns a new random callsign.

Burn does not currently erase:

  • Previous callsigns (kept so the on-device Statistics screen can still list them)
  • Play entitlement timestamps (so a paid year is not lost)
  • Tutorial “already seen” flags

Those leftovers stay on that phone only. They are not uploaded to us. If you need a full local wipe, uninstall the app after Burn. Copies already on other phones are unaffected.

10. Security — honest limits

Local storage uses the Android app sandbox; it is not extra at-rest encryption. Use a device lock screen. Paid Bluetooth mesh uses ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM per link. LoRa is not that. Capture is an ordinary web request. Cached pages do not hide later live visits. Optional share-passwords protect a payload only if both people know the phrase.

Do not treat Lantern as a VPN, Tor, Signal, Meshtastic, anonymity network, warrant-proof archive, or 911 replacement.

11. Legal bases (EEA / UK / similar)

Where those laws apply to processing that actually occurs on the device:

  • Contract / requested service — storing what you save; mesh, LoRa, maps, and controls you turn on.
  • Legitimate interests — local entitlement cache; on-device statistics; GPS/SIM lock so the radio stays on a listed unlicensed band.
  • Consent — optional profile, map share, weather, live Ask, LoRa attestation and waiver.
  • Legal obligation — if a lawful request is made about the developer; we hold no user library.

Google Play is an independent controller for your Google account and payments. Nearby Connections runs in Google Play services on the device. Weather, capture targets, and AI providers are independent controllers for the requests you send them.

12. International processing

The developer does not host user content. Capture, Play, weather, and optional AI providers may process data in other countries under their own terms.

13. Changes

14. Play Data safety summary

Accurate answers for this build:

  • Collected by the developer? No. No developer server for library, chat, or location.
  • Location? Yes, processed and stored on device (mesh, maps, LoRa lock). Not sent to us. May be shared peer-to-peer if you enable map share.
  • Device or other IDs? Callsign and channel are local. Previous callsigns may remain after Burn (on device).
  • Photos / files? Only what you import. Stay on device unless you offer them.
  • Web history? Only URLs you capture, on device.
  • Sold / ads? No.
  • Shared by the developer with third parties? No. Google Play, sites you capture, weather, and AI keys are services you contact.
  • Encrypted in transit to developer servers? Not applicable. Capture uses HTTPS when the site does. Paid Bluetooth mesh uses AES-256-GCM. LoRa is not encrypted.
  • Deletion? On-device delete / Burn / uninstall. The developer has no copy. Burn does not remove leftover callsigns or Play timestamps until uninstall.
